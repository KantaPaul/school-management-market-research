# School management market research

Evidence-based decision on whether a solo developer should build a School Management System.

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS.** Do not build a general school ERP. Interview Dhaka coaching-centre owners first. Build only a hosted fee-and-attendance product if at least two independent owners agree to a paid pilot. Otherwise stop.

The full decision, sources, and gates are in [docs/market-research/school-management/README.md](docs/market-research/school-management/README.md).

Research date: 24 October 2026. This repository contains research documents only. It does not contain application code.
